# Seed Stage Pitch Kit

## About

Generate the complete set of seed-stage fundraising materials for a startup — long-form investment memo, TEA & market sizing, financial model, live pitch deck, email deck, intro/outreach emails, and a tiered investor list.

**Use this skill when:**
- A founder wants to build, draft, refine, or pressure-test any fundraising deliverable
- You need to write an investor memo, build a pitch deck, or draft outreach emails
- You're sizing markets, building a seed financial model, or creating an investor list
- A founder pastes deck content, traction data, customer quotes, or financial models and wants them turned into investor-facing materials

**Trigger phrases:**
- "write our investor memo"
- "build a pitch deck"
- "draft an intro email to a VC"
- "size our market / TAM"
- "build a seed financial model"
- "make an investor list"
- "turn our deck into a memo"

**What it produces:**
The skill encodes a seven-deliverable system, built comprehensively-to-concisely:

1. **Long-Form Memo** (6–8 pages) — the investment thesis your champion pitches internally
2. **TEA & Market Sizing** — technical and economic analysis proving the market exists and the path works
3. **Financial Model** — a 5–6 tab spreadsheet (1,000+ formulas) showing unit economics and runway
4. **Email Deck** (10–15 slides) — stands alone in an inbox, no presenter
5. **Live Pitch Deck** (15–25 slides + appendix) — guides a 25–30 minute conversation
6. **Intro & Outreach Emails** — warm intros, cold outreach, and follow-up sequences
7. **Investor List** — tiered tracker with 40–80 targets, prioritized by fit

**Platform flexibility:**
Output can be generated in Notion, Google Workspace (Docs/Slides/Sheets), Microsoft Office (Word/PowerPoint/Excel), or Canva (for decks). Ask once where the team wants materials to live, then remember for the session.

**Key principles:**
- **Build comprehensive-to-concise:** memo forces full thinking; every shorter format is compression, not invention
- **Two-register discipline:** sober near-term claims (grounded in actuals) + earned long-term vision (connected to the near-term via TEA trajectory and market-segment unlocks)
- **Data-forward:** every claim earns a number, customer name, or citation; inline review flags for unverified claims
- **Number sync:** changes to any figure propagate to every material that references it
- **Iterate, never one-shot:** generate v1, pressure-test with a skeptical investor, refine, get human feedback, final polish

**Before you start:**
The single biggest lever on quality is what you bring to the table. Spend 30 minutes gathering existing materials (deck, investor updates, one-pagers), traction & customer proof, financials, market context, team bios, round details, and investor context. Rough is fine; messy deck + back-of-envelope model beat nothing.
